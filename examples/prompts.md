# Prompts that work

Paste any of these into a client that has the ReelMint server connected. The assistant will call `list_models` or a `quote_*` tool first and tell you the price before it spends.

## One clip, posted

```
Make a 10-second Kling 3.0 clip of a lighthouse keeper lighting the lamp at dusk, waves below, warm film grain, 9:16. Then post it to my TikTok with a caption and three hashtags.
```

## A multi-scene film with a cast

```
Plan a 4-scene, 40-second film starring @Lami and @Thiago: a street-food stall at night, they argue about the last skewer, it ends with them sharing it. Film it on Seedance 2.5 with sound, merge the scenes, and share the result to the community.
```

## Photos to film

```
Take the 12 photos I uploaded this week and make a 60-second film in the "Road trip" mood with music. Show me the quote first.
```

## A bot that posts every morning

```
Quote a bot that posts one sing-along learning song for 3 to 6 year olds to my YouTube channel every day at 08:00 Dubai time, with a Short of the chorus. If it is under 400 credits a run, create it paused and run it once now so I can see one.
```

Check whether @luna is prepared to go live. If not, prepare her. Then quote a 120-minute music stream on my YouTube channel and start it.
